Talk to us

Questions

The things people ask before they walk in.

Mostly variations on one question: am I going to embarrass myself? You aren't.

Do I need to be fit before I join?

No, and that's not a polite answer — Foundations exists precisely because most people arrive unable to squat to depth or hold a bar overhead. Six weeks later they can. If you could already do it all, you wouldn't need coaching.

What actually happens in my first session?

Your first hour is one-on-one with a coach, not a class. You'll talk through what you want and anything that hurts, then learn the four movements everything else is built on — squat, hinge, press, pull — with an empty bar. You leave knowing the vocabulary, so your first group class isn't a guessing game.

How much is membership, and is there a joining fee?

Memberships run $79, $129 and $229 a month. There is no joining fee, no lock-in contract and no minimum term — you can cancel from your account in about thirty seconds. Most members are on Brigade at $129.

What should I wear and bring?

Whatever you already own that you can move in, and flat shoes rather than running trainers if you have them. Bring a water bottle. That's the entire list — everything else lives here.

I'm coming back from an injury. Can I train?

Usually yes, and it's one of the most common reasons people start with us. Tell your coach in the intro session and the program gets written around it. Priya's background is physio, so if something needs working around rather than through, that call gets made by someone qualified to make it.

How many people are in a class?

Twelve at most, one coach. That cap is the whole model — it's the number at which a coach can still watch every rep and correct it, which is the thing you're actually paying for.

Can I try it before I pay anything?

Yes — a full week, unlimited classes, one-on-one intro session included, no card details taken. Book it from the form at the bottom of this page.
